# Store presentation quality gate

The store page should communicate the real game within seconds. Every item below requires a manual review at its final display size.

## Screenshot sequence

1. Lead with `02-pulsar-signal-rush.png`: player frame, multiple enemy silhouettes, weapons, HUD, and a distinctive background state are visible together.
2. Follow with `05-carrier-boss-encounter.png`: the boss scale and health bar establish the run objective.
3. Use `03-rift-crossfire.png` to show environmental variety.
4. Use `04-supernova-siege.png` to show late-run pressure.
5. Keep `01-deep-space-assault.png` last as the cleanest early-run view.

## Manual visual review

- no concept art is presented as gameplay;
- no feature appears in copy or imagery unless it exists in the uploaded build;
- ships retain clean alpha edges with no dark rectangular matte;
- HUD labels remain readable in Steam's reduced screenshot preview;
- the player, hostile projectiles, and boss telegraphs separate clearly from the background;
- screenshots do not repeat the same composition without demonstrating a different state;
- capsule logos remain legible at native size and obey Valve's text restrictions;
- trailer footage is recorded from the same release candidate submitted for review.

## Commercial review

- the first trailer beat shows movement, automatic weapons, and Phase Dash without a long logo intro;
- the short description names the genre, 12-minute structure, frame count, and build system without unsupported superlatives;
- the first two screenshots communicate both ordinary combat and the boss objective;
- pricing and launch discount are set only after comparable-title research and external playtest feedback;
- the store page uses one visual identity: deep navy, cyan systems, white hulls, and restrained red threat accents.
